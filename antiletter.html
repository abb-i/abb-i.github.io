01 / What it is

A letter against the default way of spending a mind.

The premise

Most of what reaches you was built to be finished, not to be remembered. Anti Letter is the other kind of reading — the sort that costs you ten minutes and stays for a month.

The subjects

Attention and who owns it. Taste, and the fact that you rent yours. Discipline that survives past February. The life that's still waiting outside the screen.

The rhythm

Roughly weekly, when there's something worth the send. Sometimes an essay, sometimes a conversation. Never a newsletter about newsletters.
