IMAGES & MEDIA REQUIRED — ABBI PERSONAL WEBSITE
================================================
Place all files in the paths listed below (relative to the project root).

────────────────────────────────────────────────
HERO SECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Path:   public/video/hero.mp4
What:   A looping personal video of you for the hero background.
Notes:  10–30 seconds ideal. Horizontal (landscape) orientation preferred.
        Will be overlaid over the site background color until added.
        Compress for web — aim for < 10 MB.

────────────────────────────────────────────────
ABOUT SECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Path:   public/images/just_me.png
What:   A photo of you with the background fully removed (transparent PNG).
Notes:  Waist-up or full-body works. Natural, relaxed pose preferred.
        Minimum 600 × 800 px. PNG with alpha channel (transparent bg).

────────────────────────────────────────────────
PORTFOLIO GRID — LOGOS
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Path:   public/images/nodistractionlogo.png
What:   Logo or cover artwork for "no distraction." podcast.
Notes:  Square format recommended (1:1 ratio). Min 800 × 800 px.

Path:   public/images/unscrolledlogo.png
What:   Logo or cover artwork for the "unscrolled" project.
Notes:  Square format recommended (1:1 ratio). Min 800 × 800 px.

Path:   public/images/antiletterlogo.png
What:   Logo or cover artwork for "antiletter" newsletter.
Notes:  Square format recommended (1:1 ratio). Min 800 × 800 px.
        ALSO used in the footer logo row.

────────────────────────────────────────────────
PORTFOLIO GRID — HOVER PREVIEW VIDEOS
────────────────────────────────────────────────
These 5-second videos play when a user hovers over each portfolio card.
They should feel like a quick, cinematic teaser for each project.

Path:   public/video/nodistraction_preview.mp4
What:   ~5 second preview clip for "no distraction." podcast.
Notes:  Could be a clip of you recording, the logo animating, or a scene
        that captures the podcast vibe. Vertical or square works best.
        Compress for web — aim for < 5 MB. Loop point should feel seamless.

Path:   public/video/unscrolled_preview.mp4
What:   ~5 second preview clip for the "unscrolled" project.
Notes:  Something that conveys phone-free living, nature, or focus.
        Compress for web — aim for < 5 MB.

Path:   public/video/antiletter_preview.mp4
What:   ~5 second preview clip for "antiletter" newsletter.
Notes:  Could be writing, reading, a typewriter, or the logo in motion.
        Compress for web — aim for < 5 MB.

────────────────────────────────────────────────
NEWSLETTER SECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Path:   public/images/antilettertransparent.png
What:   The antiletter logo on a fully transparent background.
Notes:  PNG with alpha channel. The logo will show on the dark red (#820A00)

        Same dimensions as antiletterlogo.png preferred (square, min 400px).
